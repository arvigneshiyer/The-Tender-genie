Automated screening, not legal advice. This tool flags likely clause presence/absence using text-pattern matching against common Indian public-procurement norms (GFR 2017, CVC, GeM, MSME, Make in India Order 2017, Arbitration & Conciliation Act). Rule thresholds (EMD %, PBG %, etc.) change over time — a qualified procurement officer should confirm every "Non-compliant" or "Needs review" item against the current circulars before final decisions.
